# User Persona – Daniel Watson

**Age:** 14 Years Old

**Education:** Year 9 Secondary School Student

**Location:** Manchester, UK

**Technical Confidence:** Moderate

**Devices Used:**
• Personal smartphone (daily use)
• School laptop / Chromebook
• Family desktop computer

## Background

Daniel is a 14-year-old secondary school student who spends a large portion of his free time online. He regularly uses social media, watches gaming content creators, communicates with friends through messaging applications, and plays multiplayer games after school.

Although Daniel has grown up around technology and feels confident using digital platforms, cybersecurity knowledge is mostly limited to what he has learned informally through school, family members, or personal experiences online.

## Behaviour Patterns

• Uses the internet multiple times throughout the day

• Clicks links shared by friends, gaming communities, or social media pages without always checking legitimacy

• Frequently downloads game modifications, software tools, or educational resources online

• Prefers learning through interactive experiences rather than reading long explanations

• Often multitasks while gaming, messaging friends, and browsing content simultaneously

## Goals

• Wants games that feel rewarding and challenging rather than educational

• Enjoys competitive experiences that create urgency or pressure

• Wants immediate feedback when making mistakes

• Likes progression systems such as earning points and improving scores

## Frustrations

• Long tutorials or excessive reading before gameplay begins

• Complex controls or confusing menus

• Educational content that feels disconnected from gameplay

• Losing progress after spending significant time playing

## Motivations

Daniel is motivated by achievement systems, competition, progression, and challenge. He enjoys improving performance and comparing scores with previous attempts. Fast-paced gameplay and time pressure increase engagement because they create a sense of accomplishment when challenges are completed successfully.

## Cybersecurity Risks Relevant to Daniel

Because Daniel regularly interacts with online communities, downloads content, and receives messages from unfamiliar sources, he is more likely to encounter:

• Suspicious links

• Fake login pages

• Scam advertisements

• Clickbait content

• Social engineering attempts

## Design Implications

This persona directly influenced several design decisions:

• Short learning segments were used instead of lengthy tutorials

• Educational feedback appears immediately after decisions are made

• Difficulty gradually increases to support skill development

• Retro arcade mechanics and chase sequences maintain engagement during learning activities

• Points and hint systems encourage repeated play while reducing frustration
