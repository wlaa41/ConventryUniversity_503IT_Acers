# CLICKBAIT Game – Narration Script

## Introduction

In today's digital world, phishing attacks and online scams have become increasingly common. Many people struggle to identify suspicious emails, fake websites, and dangerous links before it's too late.

To help address this issue, I developed a retro-style educational game that teaches players how to recognise phishing attempts and practice safe online behaviour through interactive gameplay.

---

## Game Story

The player takes control of a character trapped inside a mysterious maze. Lurking within the maze is a dangerous angler fish, representing online threats, phishing scams, and deceptive clickbait that constantly pursue the player.

The player's goal is to escape the maze by reaching the final exit before being caught.

---

## Gameplay

Progress through the maze is blocked by a series of locked doors.

To unlock each door, players must answer online safety questions within a limited time. These challenges include identifying phishing emails, spotting suspicious links, recognising fake login pages, and understanding unsafe online behaviour.

As players advance deeper into the maze, the questions become more difficult, testing their knowledge and decision-making skills.

---

## Pressure and Challenge

To create tension and simulate real-world decision making, the angler fish continues moving through the maze even while the player is answering questions.

This creates a sense of urgency, forcing players to think quickly while under pressure.

Correct answers unlock doors, reward points, and allow the player to continue progressing through the maze.

---

## Consequences

Incorrect answers have consequences.

The enemy may move faster, the available response time may decrease, or other gameplay penalties may be applied, increasing the difficulty of survival.

If the player runs out of time or is caught by the angler fish, they must restart and try again.

---

## Reward System

Players earn points for successfully answering questions and progressing through the maze.

These points can be spent on hints, providing additional support when faced with more difficult cybersecurity challenges.

This reward system encourages both learning and strategic resource management.

---

## Educational Purpose

The primary educational goal of this project is to improve phishing recognition skills and online safety awareness.

Every question is followed by educational feedback explaining why an answer is correct or incorrect. This ensures that players learn from both their successes and mistakes.

By repeatedly practising these skills in a challenging environment, players develop greater confidence in recognising online threats and making safer decisions on the internet.

---

## Visual Style and Theme

The game uses a retro 90s arcade-inspired 2D pixel art style.

This visual approach creates a nostalgic and engaging experience while keeping the game's design simple, accessible, and easy to understand.

The pixel art aesthetic complements the fast-paced gameplay and helps maintain player engagement throughout the learning experience.

---

## Conclusion

In conclusion, this project combines education and entertainment to create an engaging cybersecurity learning experience.

By navigating the maze, escaping online threats, and answering phishing-related challenges, players improve their online safety knowledge while enjoying a challenging arcade-style adventure.

**Can you escape the maze before the dangers of the internet catch up with you?**
