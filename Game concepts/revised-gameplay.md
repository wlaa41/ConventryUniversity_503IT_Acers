Gameplay:

The player controls a character trapped inside a maze while being chased by an angler fish that represents dangerous clickbait and online threats.
To escape the maze, the player must reach the exit by progressing through multiple locked doors placed throughout the map.

Each locked door requires the player to correctly identify phishing emails, suspicious links, fake login pages, or other examples of unsafe online behaviour within a time limit.

•	Early questions are simple and focus on basic online safety. 

•	As the player moves deeper into the maze, questions become more difficult. 

•	Players have a limited time to answer each question. 

•	While answering, the angler fish continues moving through the maze, creating pressure and urgency. 

•	Hints can be purchased using earned points

If the player answers correctly, the door unlocks, points are rewarded and they can continue.
If they answer incorrectly, player faces gameplay consequences such as increasing the enemy’s speed, reducing available time. If they run out of time, they have to start over.
The player wins by successfully answering questions, navigating through the maze, and reaching the final exit before getting caught.


Educational Purpose:

The educational aim of the project is to improve phishing recognition and online safety awareness through interactive, pressure-based decision making. Each answer triggers an educational feedback pop-up explaining why answers are correct or incorrect.


Theme:

The game uses a retro 90s arcade-inspired 2D pixel art style to create a nostalgic and engaging experience for players. This style creates an engaging and nostalgic experience while keeping visuals simple and accessible.
