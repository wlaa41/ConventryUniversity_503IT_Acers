# HOW TO PLAY

Welcome to the maze.

Your mission is simple:

ESCAPE BEFORE THE ANGLER FISH CATCHES YOU.

Controls:

Move = WASD / Arrow Keys

Pause = ESC

Rules:

1. Explore the maze and find locked doors

2. Complete cybersecurity challenges to unlock doors

3. Answer before time runs out

4. Watch out — the Angler Fish never stops chasing

5. Correct answers unlock doors and reward points

6. Wrong answers make the game harder

7. Use earned points to purchase hints

8. Reach the final exit to win

Remember:

Think before clicking.

Question suspicious links.

Stay safe online.

GOOD LUCK ESCAPING!
