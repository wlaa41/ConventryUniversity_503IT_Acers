## Discussion Summary

The team reviewed the assignment requirement to create a computer-based game prototype that teaches cybersecurity or digital safety. Several possible ideas were discussed, and the team agreed that the project should be educational, interactive, and suitable for a teenage audience.

The team decided to create a **2D visual animated action/puzzle game**. The early idea was based on an escape-style game where the player must clear obstacles and answer cybersecurity questions to progress. This approach was chosen because it allows learning to be included naturally through gameplay instead of only written information.

The team selected **15–19-year-old students** as the target audience. This group was chosen because teenagers regularly use digital devices, social media, online accounts, and websites, which makes topics such as phishing, password security, online privacy, and safe browsing highly relevant to them.

## Initial Game Concept

The player is placed in a challenging environment and must escape by completing rooms, clearing obstacles, and answering cybersecurity-related questions. Cybersecurity tips and learning points will appear during the game. These tips will help the player understand risks such as phishing, unsafe files, weak passwords, and online safety mistakes.
