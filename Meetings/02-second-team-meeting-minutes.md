## Discussion Summary

The present members reviewed the initial game concept and discussed how it could be made more practical for the available time. The team agreed that the game needed a clearer progression system so the prototype could be developed in stages.

Several tools were considered for game development, visual design, and pixel-style assets. The team decided to use **Godot** for game development, **Figma** for wireframes/interface planning, and **Piskel** for pixel-style design. These tools were chosen because they support prototyping, visual planning, and simple asset creation.

The team also discussed the animation style and agreed to use a pixel-style design because it is suitable for a 2D prototype and easier to keep consistent across characters, backgrounds, and objects.
