Before You Play

This game teaches cybersecurity safety through a maze challenge. Please read and accept the safety rules before continuing.

Terms & Conditions

  • This game is for cybersecurity learning.
  • Do not enter real personal information.
  • Scores and accounts are stored locally in this browser.
  • Do not exploit bugs or modify files unfairly.
  • Game features may be improved later.

Parental Control

  • No public chat or direct messaging.
  • No real-money purchases or loot boxes.
  • Game can be paused anytime with ESC.
  • Players should not use real names.
  • Progress is stored only on this device.

How to Play + Game Rules

How to Play

  • Move using WASD or Arrow Keys.
  • Pause using ESC.
  • Only the next locked door is shown.
  • Answer cyber questions to unlock doors.
  • Correct answers give score and Cyber Coins.
  • Hints cost 1 Cyber Coin.
  • 3 Cyber Coins can revive you.
  • You can revive maximum 2 times only.

Game Rules

  • The timer starts when the game starts.
  • The timer keeps running during questions.
  • The timer only stops when the game is paused.
  • Touching walls reduces health by 2.
  • Wrong answers make the fish faster and stronger.
  • The fish buff lets it pass through walls for a short time.
  • The final door needs a strong confirmed password.
  • The final door locks behind you after you escape.

Cyber Tips

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check URLs before entering details.
  • Do not download unknown files.
  • Never share verification codes.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Do not save passwords on shared computers.

Badges

  • Cyber Expert: 14 or more correct answers.
  • Cyber Defender: 11 or more correct answers.
  • Cyber Learner: 8 or more correct answers.
  • Cyber Beginner: less than 8 correct answers.
  • Your badge appears on the final result screen.

    Cyber Safety Tips

    These tips are for young players aged 11–16. Use them in school, gaming, and social media.

    1. Think before clicking any link.
    2. Check the website address before logging in.
    3. Never share verification codes.
    4. Use strong and unique passwords.
    5. Do not reuse the same password everywhere.
    6. Ask an adult or teacher if a message feels suspicious.
    7. Do not download free hacks, cheats, or unknown files.
    8. Be careful with public WiFi when logging into accounts.
    9. Do not save passwords on school or shared computers.
    10. Do not trust messages only because they look professional.
    11. Scammers often use fear and urgency to rush you.
    12. Keep your gaming accounts protected with strong passwords.
    13. Do not share private photos, address, school name, or phone number.
    14. Report suspicious messages instead of replying to them.
    15. When unsure, stop, check, and ask for help.
