## Discussion Summary

The team reviewed the overall presentation strategy and agreed that each member must prepare and deliver an individual talk as part of Tuesday's presentation. To ensure everyone was ready and confident, a rehearsal was planned for Monday — the day before — so the team could practise their delivery and identify any gaps.

The current game status was reviewed. The team confirmed that the game is progressing and is centred on password hygiene as its core educational theme. The transparent or login-based approach was discussed and set aside in favour of focusing on the password hygiene content. The team also agreed that an animation sequence would be developed and demonstrated at the end of the game to make the final product more engaging and visually appealing.

A significant concern was raised regarding individual contributions, as some team members had not been contributing sufficiently to the project. The team agreed that all members must increase their involvement and complete their assigned work before the presentation.
